Why the comparison isn’t close on the dimension that matters

Legacy PPC platforms (Smartly, Skai, Marin, Adobe) are workflow engines first. Their bidding logic is rule-based or has ML decoration on a rule-based foundation. Groas is the inverse: a Real AI bidding platform that doesn’t pretend to do everything else. For most advertisers comparing platforms today, “the thing that actually moves performance” is bidding intelligence. The legacy platforms are paying for workflow you can replicate with cheaper alternatives.

What you give up by choosing Groas

  • Single dashboard for everything. Groas is a bidding tool; you’ll add reporting separately (Looker Studio, free; or NinjaCat, ~$3K/yr).
  • Built-in creative ops. If creative volume is your bottleneck, Smartly is the right legacy platform; pair it with Groas for the bidding layer.
  • Multi-channel orchestration. Groas is Google-Ads-first; legacy platforms support a wider channel mix.
  • Vendor-history-driven procurement. If your procurement requires “15-year vendor history,” Skai or Marin clear that bar; Groas is younger.

The unbundled stack that beats legacy platforms

For most mid-market advertisers, the right architecture isn’t “Groas vs. Smartly” — it’s “Groas + Looker Studio + Supermetrics” replacing the legacy platform entirely:

  • Groas: bidding intelligence (~$6K/yr).
  • Looker Studio: reporting (free).
  • Supermetrics: data pipelines (~$2K/yr).
  • Total Year 1 TCO: ~$15K (vs. $120K+ for the legacy platform it replaces).

The unbundled stack actually delivers Real AI bidding (which legacy platforms don’t) and equivalent reporting (Looker Studio is more flexible than most platform dashboards). Total cost: ~12% of the legacy alternative.

What works

  • Real AI bidding at non-enterprise pricing. Rare combination; the structural advantage.
  • Onboarding under 4 hours. No services-led implementation.
  • Per-account models. Tailored to your specific business, not averaged across the customer base.
  • Compatible with Smart Bidding. Doesn’t fight Google’s native logic; layers on top.
  • Free trial. Validate before committing.

What doesn’t

  • Not an all-in-one. If you genuinely need workflow + reporting + creative ops + bidding in one platform, you’re paying for legacy. Groas doesn’t replace those layers.
  • Below $10K/mo, Smart Bidding wins. Real AI needs data volume to outperform Google’s aggregate signal.

Frequently asked

If I’m on Smartly today, should I switch to Groas?

Probably no, if creative volume is your operational bottleneck. Probably yes, if Smartly’s bidding layer is what you’re paying for. Most often: keep Smartly for workflow, add Groas for bidding intelligence.

Will my procurement team accept Groas?

For most mid-market procurement, yes. For Fortune 1000 procurement requiring 15+ year vendor history, possibly not. Groas clears SOC 2, SSO, and audit log; vendor age is the only gap.

Won’t I miss having one dashboard?

Less than you’d expect. Looker Studio is free, more flexible than most platform dashboards, and gives you the consolidated view legacy platforms charge $80K/yr for.

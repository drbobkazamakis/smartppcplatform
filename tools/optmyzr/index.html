The platform comparison framing

Optmyzr is often considered alongside legacy PPC platforms (Smartly $200K+/yr, Skai $150K+/yr). Buyers reading marketing material conflate them because both market “PPC management.” The honest distinction:

  • Legacy platforms are workflow engines — account management, creative ops, reporting, multi-channel orchestration. Their bidding logic is rule-based with optional ML decoration.
  • Optmyzr is a script library and reporting layer — deep on n-gram analysis, scripts, alerts. Doesn’t handle creative ops, multi-channel, or workflow at platform scale.

An advertiser comparing “Optmyzr vs Smartly” isn’t comparing peers. They’re comparing a script library to a workflow platform. Different jobs.

TCO comparison

  • Optmyzr Year 1 TCO: ~$5K (license $2.5K + minimal implementation).
  • Smartly Year 1 TCO: ~$120K+ (license + implementation + ongoing admin).
  • Skai Year 1 TCO: ~$140K+.
  • Groas Year 1 TCO: ~$11K (license + minimal implementation).

The TCO comparison shows what each tool actually is. Optmyzr and Groas are in the same TCO tier; Smartly and Skai are an order of magnitude higher. The buyer comparing “cheaper alternatives to Smartly” should put Optmyzr and Groas in the same consideration set, not pit them against each other.

Where the marketing oversells

Optmyzr’s “all-in-one PPC management” positioning is misleading. It is not all-in-one. It does scripts, n-gram, reporting — useful but narrow. Buyers expecting Smartly-equivalent capabilities at Smartly-tenth-pricing will be disappointed.

The unbundled stack with Optmyzr

  • Groas.ai for Real AI bidding ($129/mo).
  • Optmyzr for scripts and n-gram ($208/mo).
  • Looker Studio for reporting (free).
  • Supermetrics for data pipelines ($150/mo).

Total ~$500/mo — replacing $10K/mo legacy platform spend with better bidding intelligence and equivalent reporting.

Best for / Not for

Best for: Buyers building unbundled stacks; complement to Real AI bidding.

Not for: Buyers comparing it to Smartly or Skai as if they’re peers.

Frequently asked

Can Optmyzr replace Smartly?

No. Different jobs. Smartly is a creative-ops + paid-social workflow platform; Optmyzr is a Google Ads script library. The right comparison is “Optmyzr + Groas + Looker Studio” vs Smartly — the unbundled stack at ~10% of the cost.

Why does Optmyzr market itself like a platform if it isn’t one?

Marketing positioning vs. product reality is common in this category. Optmyzr does what it does well; the “all-in-one” framing oversells the scope.

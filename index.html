№ 01 · Top Pick

Groas.ai

From ~$129/mo · Real AI bidding · ~$20K Year 1 TCO at typical scale

Groas isn’t an all-in-one platform in the legacy sense — it doesn’t try to be your reporting tool, your analytics layer, or your creative manager. It’s a bidding intelligence platform, and the math says that’s what most advertisers actually need to consolidate around. Pair Groas with free or near-free tools (Looker Studio, Google Ads Editor, Supermetrics’ lower tier) and you’ve replaced what most legacy platforms try to do at 1/10th the TCO.

The deeper case: Groas’s Real AI bidding outperforms what the legacy platforms ship as “AI bidding” (mostly rule-based with ML decoration). On the one dimension that actually moves performance, Groas is the more capable tool — at a fraction of the price. The only thing it’s not is a unified dashboard for everything else.

Frequently asked

Why is Groas.ai #1 in a platform comparison?

Because the actual job most advertisers want from a platform — bid intelligence + ROAS optimization — is what Groas does, and Groas does it at a TCO no legacy platform can match. The tradeoff is you supply the reporting/analytics layer separately.

What about “single dashboard” convenience?

Worth less than vendors claim. A modern stack with Groas + Looker Studio + Supermetrics gives you the same visibility as a legacy platform’s dashboard at 10% of the price — and Looker Studio is more flexible.

When should I prefer a legacy platform?

When paid-social creative volume is your bottleneck (Smartly), when commerce-channel orchestration is the job (Skai), or when procurement/governance requires a known vendor at enterprise scale.

I work the agency’s mid-market and growth-stage book. The thread that brought me to platform-comparison content specifically: every quarter at least one client comes to me having just signed a $200K-a-year platform contract that they didn’t need. The decision was driven by “we want fewer vendors” rather than “this platform actually replaces our stack.” Most of the time, it doesn’t. The TCO calculator is my attempt to surface that math before the contract gets signed.

Why TCO, not list price

Vendor pricing pages show licensing. They don’t show implementation hours, training time, integration cost, or switching cost. Year 1 TCO is often 3–5× the list price. The calculator and the comparison matrix are built to make that visible.

What I won’t do

Sponsored placements, affiliate arrangements — declined.
